Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you source your beans?

We source our beans directly from sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil, ensuring the highest quality and fair compensation for farmers.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship our premium beans worldwide. Delivery times vary by region but typically take 5-10 business days.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Orders can be modified within 2 hours of placement. Please contact our support team immediately if you need to make changes.
